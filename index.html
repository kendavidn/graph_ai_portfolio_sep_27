About me

Data, research & education.

PhD candidate in epidemiology, trained in data science and web development. Expert in R, proficient in Python and Javascript.

I am a researcher and data analyst at the University of Geneva, investigating risk factors of negative outcomes along the cascade of care for HIV-positive female sex workers in Nigeria.

I also co-founded and co-direct The GRAPH Courses, a non-profit education platform with over 4000 learners, where we develop and deliver courses for R-based health data analysis.

My work sits at the intersection of epidemiology, quantitative analysis, web development and teaching.

EducationPhD Candidate, Infectious Diseases & Mathematical Modelling Group, University of Geneva

UndergraduateBA in Biology (concentration in Education), Vassar College · cum laude

About the course

Responsible AI, applied.

At GRAPH Courses, the bootcamp-style approach combines academic rigor with practical, industry-relevant skills. The course focuses on real-world projects and responsible use of generative AI.

Live weekly workshops Hands-on practice Personalized feedback Dedicated office hours Certificates of completion ECTS credits for partner universities
Course modules
01
Documents, Presentations & WebsitesPrompting, document generation, simple research websites and AI presentation tools.
02
Automated Data AnalysisAI tools for quantitative data analysis, coding assistants and verification of statistical outputs.
03
GenAI for Research & Text AnalysisLiterature review, structured extraction, qualitative analysis and managing hallucinations, costs and privacy.
04
Custom GPTs and ChatbotsKnowledge bases, context windows, RAG, internal tools and safer external-facing chatbots.
05
AI Tools for Workflow AutomationTriggers, research digests, browser-control agents and keeping a human in the loop.
06
GenAI for Communication & OutreachAI-generated visuals, scientific diagrams, flyers, visual abstracts and video.
07
Capstone Project & ShowcaseCollaborate, refine a final AI solution or presentation, receive feedback and present the result.
